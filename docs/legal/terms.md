# Croppr terms of use

**DRAFT for legal review. Not yet in force.** Version 2026-10-draft-1.

These terms apply when you use the Croppr app. By creating an account you agree to them.

## What Croppr is

Croppr is a platform that helps farms and other agricultural employers find workers, and helps workers find jobs. Croppr is not the employer of any worker and is not a labour hire provider. Employment is a matter between the farm and the worker.

## Who can use Croppr

- You must be at least 18 years old.
- Workers must have the right to work in Australia for the jobs they apply for.
- Farms must be a genuine Australian business with an active ABN, and must hold any labour hire licence their state requires.
- Each person or business may hold one account. Keep your sign-in details to yourself.

## Farms' responsibilities

- Pay at least the minimum required by the relevant award (for example the Horticulture Award) and the Fair Work Act, including for piece-rate work.
- Describe jobs, pay, accommodation and conditions honestly.
- Meet your obligations as an employer, including workplace health and safety, superannuation and record keeping.
- Mark attendance honestly. Marking a worker as a no-show when they turned up is a breach of these terms.

## Workers' responsibilities

- Give accurate information about yourself, your availability and your visa.
- Turn up when you've accepted a job, or tell the farm as early as you can if you can't.

## Visa and specified work information

Croppr's day tracker and "eligible postcode" labels are estimates to help you plan. They are not immigration advice. Always check the current requirements with the Department of Home Affairs or a registered migration agent before applying for a visa.

## Ratings, reviews and reports

- Ratings and reviews must be honest and based on your own experience of that job.
- Don't post reviews that are abusive, discriminatory, false or that reveal someone's private information.
- Anyone can report a review. We may hide reviews that break these rules.
- Workers can dispute a no-show. We'll review the evidence and decide whether it stands.

## Messages and conduct

Don't harass, threaten, scam or discriminate against anyone. Don't ask workers for payments or bonds to get a job. Don't use Croppr to send spam.

## Suspension and closing accounts

We may suspend or close an account that breaks these terms or puts others at risk. You can delete your account at any time in the app.

## Liability

Croppr is provided "as is". We don't guarantee that you'll find work or workers. To the extent allowed by law, we're not liable for the acts of farms or workers, or for indirect losses. Nothing in these terms excludes rights you have under the Australian Consumer Law.

## Changes

We may update these terms. If the change is important, we'll ask you to accept the new version in the app.
