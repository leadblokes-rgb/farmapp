# Croppr privacy policy

**DRAFT for legal review. Not yet in force.** Version 2026-10-draft-1.

This policy explains how Croppr ("we", "us") handles personal information under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles.

## Who we are

Croppr is an app that connects farms and other agricultural employers in Australia with people looking for farm work. Contact us about privacy at the support email shown in the app.

## What we collect

- **Account details:** your email address and sign-in records.
- **Worker profile:** your name, photo, current postcode, regions you want to work in, dates you're available, skills, experience, whether you have a car or need accommodation, and travel group details.
- **Visa details:** your visa type, visa expiry date and nationality. We use these only to tell farms whether your visa covers a job's dates (yes or no). Farms never see your expiry date or nationality.
- **Farm details:** business name, ABN, phone number, postcode, labour hire licence details, description, logo and photos.
- **Activity:** jobs you post, swipe, save or apply for; decisions on applications; check-in records (turned up, no-show, left early); ratings and reviews; reports and disputes; chat messages.
- **Day tracker:** employers, shifts and payslips you choose to record, including payslip images or PDFs.
- **Device information:** a push notification token if you allow notifications, and basic technical information needed to run the app.

We don't collect passport numbers, dates of birth, tax file numbers or street addresses. "Use my location" turns your location into a postcode once and doesn't keep your location.

## How we use it

- To match workers with jobs and show farms a ranked shortlist of applicants.
- To let farms and workers message each other and send push notifications you've allowed.
- To estimate specified-work days in your day tracker (an estimate only, not immigration advice).
- To show ratings and reviews under the rules below.
- To verify farms, keep Croppr safe, handle reports and disputes, and enforce our terms.
- To improve Croppr, using aggregated information where possible.

We don't sell your personal information and we don't use it for third-party advertising.

## Who can see what

- **Farms you apply to** see your profile, photo, availability, a yes/no visa check, your reliability rating and no-show count, and reviews from other farms. They don't see your visa expiry, nationality or day tracker.
- **Workers** see a farm's public details, its ratings and reviews (reviewers are shown by first name only).
- **Ratings are blind:** neither side sees the other's rating for a job until both have rated, or 14 days have passed.
- **Your day tracker** (employers, shifts, payslips) is visible only to you.
- **Croppr staff** can access information when needed to verify farms, investigate reports or disputes, or meet legal obligations.

## Where it's stored and overseas disclosure

Our database and files are hosted by Supabase in Sydney, Australia. Some service providers may process limited information overseas, for example push notification delivery (Expo, United States) and email delivery. We take reasonable steps to make sure they protect it.

## How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active. When you delete your account in the app, we delete your profile, photos, payslips and day tracker. Some records (for example reports, or messages the other person still holds) may be kept for a limited time where needed for safety, disputes or legal reasons.

## Your choices and rights

- You can see and edit most of your information in the app.
- You can delete your account at any time (Profile, then Delete my account).
- You can ask us for a copy of your information or to correct it using the support email.
- You can turn off push notifications in your phone's settings.

## Complaints

If you have a privacy concern, contact us first using the support email. If you're not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au.

## Changes

If we change this policy in an important way, we'll ask you to review and accept it in the app.
