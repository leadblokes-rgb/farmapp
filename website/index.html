Whether you’re after a season’s work or hiring your next crew, Croppr puts the right people and the right farms together.

Looking for work

  • Swipe through jobs that fit your dates, location and pay.
  • Every farm is checked by our team before it can post.
  • See whether a job’s postcode counts as specified work for your 417 or 462 visa.
  • Track your days towards your 2nd or 3rd year visa, with payslips in one place.
  • Croppr is free for workers. Always.

Hiring a crew

  • Post a job in a few minutes, with a check against the Horticulture Award minimum.
  • Get a ranked shortlist: availability, distance, transport, experience and reliability.
  • Accept a whole travelling group at once and message your crew in one go.
  • Check in who turned up on day one, then rate each other.

Croppr’s day tracker is an estimate, not immigration advice. Home Affairs decides what counts as specified work.
